# Cloudwake — five bells across the Cloudsea

## Premise

You are a novice skiff fisher stranded on Bellheart Isle after its wind bell falls silent. Five island bells once marked safe currents across a deep cloud ocean. Without their notes, captains cannot read the routes. The villages need catches and supplies while trade is interrupted. You earn Crowns by fishing island waters, physically landing and dispatching catches, then selling them. Those earnings buy the equipment needed to recover each island's missing bell component.

Fishing is your livelihood, not an arbitrary quest toll. Shops remain useful after the local story ends. Each restored bell unlocks a charted voyage and helps its community; the player chooses when to leave and may return to fish. No repeated compulsory catch quota is planned.

## Bellheart: the first note

1. Orin explains the closed route and directs the stranded fisher to Mira.
2. Mira loans a rod because her village needs food. The pond teaches casting, reeling and handling a physical catch.
3. Selling pays for Bram's knife. Sharpening is optional preparation, not a story gate.
4. The tower's missing socket and bronze claw marks reveal the culprit.
5. Bellcrab collects resonant metal in its shell. Mira's bell-scented bait draws it out into a clear arena.
6. Defeating it releases the physical Bellheart. Carry it uphill and reinstall it.
7. The bell restores a safe current. Tavi prepares the skiff and identifies the next destination.

## World and travel rules

- Fish live in real ponds, streams and lagoons on the floating islands. Cloudsea is a separate atmospheric space, not blue water surrounding an ordinary ground-level island.
- Cloud skiffs are flying sailing boats supported by lift keels. Sails steer along bell-marked air currents above and through clouds. Dock moorings connect to the island edge.
- Final Cloudsea needs layered white cumulus, atmospheric distance and a readable island underside. Never place collision on clouds or imply players can walk on them. Current broad sphere masses are depth placeholders only; volumetric art and sailing remain later production stages.
- Falling or defeat activates a rescue tether linked to the dock. Current prototype returns the player to the dock, restores health and preserves Crowns and carried equipment. An active boss resets so it cannot be chipped down through repeated rescues. Quest objects are recoverable; an independently dropped Bellheart below the island returns near the dock.
- Future rescue animation should show tether light, a brief cloud fade and dock arrival. No punitive money loss is planned for the first island. Do not label this future animation as implemented.

## Interaction presentation

NPC dialogue belongs beside/above the visible speaker, with a name, wrapped text and a small pointer. Walking away hides it; looking behind does not leave a floating speech box on the HUD. System feedback remains separate. Gameplay stays active. A later dialogue pass can introduce player-advanced pages; current prototype uses timed bubbles. Shops will use the physical counter and a compact item card, not a full-screen catalogue obscuring the merchant.

## Implemented in this revision

Rewritten opening/objectives/NPC dialogue, post-restoration responses, speaker-anchored wrapped bubbles, explanatory rescue feedback, and non-colliding Cloudsea depth placeholders. No additional islands, final clouds, boat sailing or final assets are claimed complete.
