# Cloudwake visual direction

Source references: `references/wind-bell-harbor.png` and `references/market-gameplay.png`.

First-island interpretation: warm cream masonry, teal cloth, golden wood, green meadows, a central bell landmark, a fairy merchant, a short dock and a tiny open-deck flying boat. The view is a live third-person world with an orbiting title camera. It deliberately starts with simpler geometry than the AI concept illustrations.

Current editable scene: `Cloudwake-Harbor.blend`. Browser mesh export: `../public/models/harbor.glb`. Browser runtime keeps the player, boat and quest actors dynamic. Terrain and static shops load from the Blender export.

Next art pass: asymmetric island outline and walkable elevation, richer tree silhouette, stylized rock strata, more expressive character rigs, cloth animation, cloud depth, camera collision and soundscape. Keep the navigation route and readable shop fronts clear. Fairy Lantern Grove is the next level, after the harbor feels good to play.
